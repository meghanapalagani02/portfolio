Quantum AI Demand Forecasting & QAOA Production Optimization

Smart Manufacturing (UC-062) multi-constraint production planning pipeline.

Developed as a comprehensive Industry 4.0 solution connecting time-series demand forecasting with quantum optimization algorithms. Cleans and pre-processes manufacturing demand data to train classical regression models and compare them against Qiskit Variational Quantum Regressors (VQR). Forecasted demand feeds into a manufacturing constraints matrix (machine capacity, inventory cost, shortage penalties), formulated as a QUBO and solved using QAOA algorithms with an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

Classical vs. Quantum ML Comparison: Evaluates Linear Regression, Random Forest, and Qiskit VQR on MAE/RMSE.
Constraint QUBO Formulation: Incorporates capacity, procurement costs, and stockout penalties into binary quadratic models.
